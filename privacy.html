Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026 • Effective: 18 May 2026

FD-DAB ("the Bot", "we", "us", "our") is a Discord analytics bot operated by FarkusDynamics. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use FD-DAB. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and applicable international data protection laws.

🔒 Summary: We collect Discord message metadata (counts, timestamps, channel activity) to generate analytics. We do not read or store private message content. We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes. You can request data deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Discord Server Data

When FD-DAB is added to a Discord server, we collect the following data to provide analytics services:

  • Server metadata: Server ID, name, member count
  • Message activity: Author ID, channel ID, timestamp, word count — not the content of messages (except keyword tracking matched keywords and ghost ping deleted content, which are stored temporarily)
  • Voice activity: User ID, join/leave timestamps, channel ID, session duration
  • Reactions: Emoji usage counts per user per message
  • Member events: Join, leave, role change timestamps
  • Interaction data: Slash command usage for improving the Bot's features

1.2 Support Data

If you contact us via email at fd-dab-bot@agentmail.to, we collect your email address, name, and the content of your message for customer support purposes.

1.3 Website Analytics

Our website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies or collect personal data. It provides anonymous, aggregate usage data (page views, referrers, browser types). No personally identifiable information is collected.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively for:

  • Generating server analytics, statistics, and PDF reports
  • Providing activity heatmaps, streaks, and leaderboards
  • Detecting ghost pings and tracking keywords you configure
  • Sending scheduled reports to channels you designate
  • Improving the Bot's features and performance
  • Responding to support inquiries

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We may share data only:

  • With Discord: As required by Discord's API terms and applicable law
  • With service providers: Our infrastructure (hosting, email, analytics) uses third-party services that process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements
  • If required by law: To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or lawful government requests

4. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained based on your subscription tier:

  • Free tier: 7 days
  • Pro tier: 30 days
  • Business tier: 90 days

When the Bot is removed from a server, all associated analytics data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months for customer service purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure API authentication using bot tokens
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized systems only
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure

6. Your Rights (POPIA & GDPR)

Under POPIA and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about your server
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your server's data (remove the Bot from your server and email us)
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data (remove the Bot)
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at fd-dab-bot@agentmail.to.

7. Children's Privacy

FD-DAB is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

FD-DAB integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Discord API — For bot functionality and data retrieval
  • AgentMail — For support inbox email handling
  • Plausible Analytics — For privacy-friendly website analytics
  • GitHub Pages — For website hosting

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be communicated via the Bot's `/dab help` command.
